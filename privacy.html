Privacy & Security

Split Happens — in plain language, no legalese.

← Back to Split Happens

The short version

Your files never leave your device, and we collect nothing about you. There is no account, no tracking, no analytics, and no server that could see your PDFs — because there is no server. This whole site is just static files.

What happens to your PDFs

  • Split Happens mobile (/mobile/): your PDFs are processed inside your browser using the open-source pdf-lib library. Merging, splitting — it all happens on your phone.
  • Split Happens full app: a Rust program compiled to WebAssembly that runs locally on your device, including offline after the first load.
  • Nothing is uploaded, emailed, or sent anywhere. If you turn off your Wi-Fi after the first load, everything still works — try it.

What we collect: nothing

  • No accounts or sign-ups.
  • No analytics, trackers, or advertising cookies.
  • We don't know who you are, what files you open, or what you do with them.

What the hosting provider sees

Honesty corner: these pages are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it inevitably sees basic technical logs (your IP address, which pages were requested). We don't have access to those logs, don't want them, and couldn't tie them to your files if we tried — your files never travel over the network at all.

What's stored on your device

  • App files are cached by your browser (via a service worker) so the app starts fast and works offline.
  • The full app may keep settings and a recent-files list in your browser's local storage.
  • Clearing the site's data in your browser settings removes all of it.

Optional cloud features

The full app can connect to cloud storage providers you configure. That's off by default. If you turn it on, that provider's privacy policy applies to whatever you choose to send it.

Security, honestly

  • Split Happens is open source (MIT / Apache-2.0) — anyone can inspect exactly what it does.
  • It's early-stage software and hasn't had a formal security audit. Because your files never leave your device, the realistic risk is bugs, not surveillance — but for extremely sensitive documents, use your judgment as you would with any young software.
  • Found a problem? Report it on GitHub.
